How to count fixtures and devices on electrical plans

Start from the legend and fixture schedule, count one symbol at a time across a whole sheet, and mark each as you go, so what is left unmarked is what you have missed. Then compare your totals with the schedule and the panel schedules.

  1. Read the legend and the abbreviations. The same circle can be a receptacle, a junction box or a smoke detector on different jobs.
  2. Split by type letter. A 2×4 troffer tagged A and one tagged A-EM are two items.
  3. One symbol, one whole sheet. Jumping between symbols is how devices are counted twice.
  4. Mark what you count. On paper a highlighter; in an app, a mark on each one, or the counted ones covered up.
  5. Watch for repeats. Enlarged plans, unit plans and typical floors show the same devices again. Count a typical unit once and multiply.
  6. Check details and risers for devices no floor plan shows: rooftop disconnects, panels, fire alarm risers.
  7. Compare with the fixture schedule's types: a type with no count is the first place to look.

Questions

Can software count every symbol by itself?

Not reliably. Treat what it proposes as a first pass and check it, above all on scans.

How do I handle typical floors?

Count one and multiply, and note which sheets you multiplied.

Why don't my counts match the schedule?

Common causes are enlarged plans counted twice, alternates, and devices shown only on a riser or detail.
