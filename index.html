Count, measure, mark up and price construction plan PDFs on iPad, iPhone and Mac. New here? Start with the steps below, then see the user guide for every feature.

Contact

Email with a description of the problem and your device. A screenshot, or the sheet you were working on, helps a lot.

Getting started

  1. Tap + and import your plan set PDF from Files.
  2. Set the sheet's scale: pick it from the scale menu, or calibrate from any dimension on the sheet.
  3. Pick an item, then tap to count, or trace to measure lengths and areas.
  4. To count a symbol across the sheet, zoom in, turn on Find all and tap one copy of it. Check the matches, then tap Add. All sheets then counts it on the rest of the set.
  5. Mark up with Markup: clouds, callouts, notes, the Apple Pencil pen, stamps and site photos.
  6. Give items a price, add overhead and profit under the total, and export to Excel, CSV or a PDF report.

Common questions

Find all asks me to zoom in. At full-sheet zoom a fingertip covers several symbols. Zoom in until the symbol is easy to tap.

Find all missed some symbols, or found the wrong ones. It works best on PDFs exported from CAD. Scanned paper drawings work less well. Every match is shown for you to check before it is added, and you can tap to add or erase marks by hand.

Where are my plans stored? Only on your device. The app has no accounts and uploads nothing.

How do I cancel Tally Pro? In Settings > your name > Subscriptions on iPhone or iPad, or in the App Store app on Mac. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I bought Tally Pro on another device. One subscription covers iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple Account. If it doesn't show, open the paywall and tap Restore purchases.

My finger moves the sheet instead of drawing with Pen. Once an Apple Pencil has drawn, only the Pencil draws, so fingers can move the sheet (as in Notes). With iPadOS's Only Draw with Apple Pencil off and no Pencil used, a finger draws.

Searching a scanned set takes a while the first time. Scans have no text layer, so Tally reads each scanned sheet once (a few seconds a sheet) and keeps the result with the takeoff. Later searches are instant.

Where are my photos? Inside the takeoff, on your device. They appear at the end of the PDF report and the plan PDF export; tap a photo pin with Move to see or share one.

How do I get my markups into Bluebeam? Export Plan PDF with markups. Every mark, markup and stamp is a standard PDF annotation, so Bluebeam and Acrobat list and edit them.

Hide counted symbols covers too much or too little. Symbols counted with Find all are covered at their own size; ones you tapped by hand get a fixture-sized cover. Turn it off from the item menu at any time.
