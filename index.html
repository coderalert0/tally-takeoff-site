Getting started

  1. Tap + and import your plan set PDF from Files.
  2. Set the sheet's scale: pick it from the scale menu, or calibrate from any dimension on the sheet.
  3. Pick an item, then tap to count, or trace to measure lengths and areas.
  4. To count a symbol across the sheet, zoom in, turn on Find all and tap one copy of it. Check the matches, then tap Add.

Common questions

Find all asks me to zoom in. At full-sheet zoom a fingertip covers several symbols. Zoom in until the symbol is easy to tap.

Find all missed some symbols, or found the wrong ones. It works best on PDFs exported from CAD. Scanned paper drawings work less well. Every match is shown for you to check before it is added, and you can tap to add or erase marks by hand.

Where are my plans stored? Only on your device. The app has no accounts and uploads nothing.

How do I cancel Tally Pro? In Settings > your name > Subscriptions on iPhone or iPad, or in the App Store app on Mac. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I bought Tally Pro on another device. Open the paywall and tap Restore purchases.
