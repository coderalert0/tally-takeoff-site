Privacy Policy

Your plans stay yours

Construction Takeoff - Tally does not collect, store or share any personal data. Last updated October 16, 2026.

  • Plan PDFs you import, and the takeoffs, marks, notes and photos you make, are stored on your device and, when your device is signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. You can turn iCloud off in the app's list of takeoffs. We never receive them: they go only to Apple's iCloud, under your account.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not contact any server of ours.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details.
  • Files you export (CSV, Excel, PDF or takeoff files) go wherever you choose to save or share them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
