Construction Takeoff - Tally does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • Plan PDFs you import, and the takeoffs, marks and notes you make, are stored only on your device. The app never uploads them.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not contact any server of ours.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details.
  • Files you export (CSV or PDF) go wherever you choose to save or share them.
