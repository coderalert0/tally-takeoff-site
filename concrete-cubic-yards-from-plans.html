How to get cubic yards of concrete from a plan

Cubic yards = area in square feet × depth in inches ÷ 324. A 4 inch slab takes one cubic yard for every 81 square feet. (324 is 27 cubic feet in a yard times 12 inches in a foot.)

Footings, walls and pads

ElementMeasure on the planCubic yards
Continuous footingLength in feetlength × width (ft) × depth (ft) ÷ 27
Foundation wallLength in feetlength × height (ft) × thickness (in) ÷ 324
Pad or pier footingCountcount × length × width × depth (all in ft) ÷ 27
Round pierCountcount × 0.7854 × diameter² (ft) × depth (ft) ÷ 27

Example: a footing 120 ft long, 24 in wide and 12 in deep is 120 × 2 × 1 ÷ 27 = 8.89 cubic yards. A wall 120 ft long, 8 ft high and 8 in thick is 120 × 8 × 8 ÷ 324 = 23.70.

Before you order

  1. Take thickened edges, turn-downs and haunches separately; they are not in the slab's area.
  2. Subtract openings and pits, not small sleeves.
  3. Add waste for spillage, uneven grade and over-excavation. The amount is your judgment and depends on the subgrade; keep it as its own line.
  4. Round up to what the plant delivers.

In Tally

Outline the slab, give the area item a depth in inches and it shows cubic yards, priced per yard if you like. Cut-outs are subtracted. Footings are runs and pads are counts.

Questions

How many square feet does a yard of concrete cover at 4 inches?

81 square feet.

And at 6 inches?

54 square feet.

Why divide by 324?

27 cubic feet make a cubic yard and 12 inches make a foot: 27 × 12 = 324.
